# Groove Grid Roadmap (deferred beyond v1)

Rough priority order. Each item should arrive as a new layer or mode on the v1 grid, not a rewrite.

1. **Tap mode:** the musician taps the screen or keyboard on markers, and timestamped input shows early/late. Gives real feedback without a mic and covers practice without an instrument.
2. **Recording + playback** against the click. The waveform is drawn around the grid so hits are visible against the lines. Stacked loops can be drawn like tree rings: tight rings mean in the pocket.
3. **Click ladder / gap click.** The ladder fades support level by level (16ths → quarters → 2 & 4 → beat 1 → silence). The gap click plays N bars, drops out for N, then returns with a "moment of truth" cue.
4. **Settings in URL:** restores the last state and makes patterns shareable (teacher → student, band "challenge of the week").
5. **Swing ratio:** shifts off-beat subdivisions for swing or laid-back feel. Markers stay attached to grid positions.
   **Line layout:** a straight grid where the dot jumps back to 1, as an alternative to the circle.
   **Compound meters:** beat grouping (6/8 as 2×3, 7/8 as 2+2+3) and custom accents.
6. **Tempo ramp** (gradual speed-up/slow-down), **call-and-response** (the app plays the marker pattern for one bar and stays silent the next while you play), **per-marker sounds** (mini drum machine), **backing tracks**.
7. **Progress:** BPM personal-best graph, daily groove + streaks, ghosts of best takes.
8. **Auto-correction:** the app listens and scores timing.

## Judged too big for now

- Live ghost (Trackmania-style: best run's taps or waveform shown faintly during live play).
- Teacher features.
- Tempo ramp.
- Gamification.

## Risky; experiment only

- Haptic/smartwatch beat output: vibration motor and Bluetooth latency/jitter conflict with rock-solid timing.
