# UX Candidates (unconfirmed)

> History only. The UX run decided these in `EXPERIENCE.md`, which is authoritative.

Coach-proposed ideas from the brainstorm that the user neither adopted nor rejected. They are input for the UX assessment, not commitments. Each one must pass the no-gimmicks and minimal-UI constraints in SPEC.md.

## Tempo control (relates to CAP-5)

- Slider or +/− buttons (user: "all possible").
- Jog-wheel drag around the circle.
- Tap tempo, only for an initial value (user: never very precise).
- Arrow/+− keys, with Shift = ±5.
- A tempo change takes effect on the next downbeat.

## Transport and session (CAP-8)

- "Zero to groove in 2s": opens with the last settings, one tap or the spacebar starts, no loading, setup or account.
- Controls fade on play, leaving only the grid, dot and BPM. A touch brings them back.
- One intention per session (e.g. "hit the 'a' of 2 at 85 BPM").

## Markers (CAP-6, CAP-7)

- Tap to place, drag to move, tap to remove.
- ~~Long-press off-grid nudge~~: rejected, markers stay on the grid (swing ratio is on the roadmap).
- Anticipation glow: the marker brightens as the dot approaches and peaks on the hit.

## Peripheral readability (CAP-2)

- Comet tail: the dot leaves a fading trail to convey speed and position.
- Subtle whole-screen pulse on beat 1.
